Privacy Policy

English

BanglaConnect is designed to help people connect through random video chat. We use information needed to provide and protect the service, including account information and technical data required for authentication, safety, and operation.

Do not share passwords, financial information, home addresses, or other sensitive personal information with strangers. We do not promise that conversations with other users are private or that another user cannot capture content using an external device.

If you have questions about privacy, contact the developer using the information in Developer Information.
