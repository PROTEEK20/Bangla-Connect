Privacy Policy / প্রাইভেসি পলিসি

1. English Version

Welcome to Bangla Connect. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. We use Firebase Authentication to secure user logins. Our video chat feature utilizes peer-to-peer (WebRTC) technology, meaning your audio and video streams are exchanged directly between participants and are never stored on our servers. We may collect basic region or IP data for regional matching services and online user counts.
